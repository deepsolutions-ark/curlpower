ABOUT

Personalized haircare and education for every texture, age and culture.

Curl Power is a curl-specialist studio in downtown Rochester, tucked inside The Metropolitan Building on South Clinton Avenue. Curl cuts, haircuts, colour, blowouts and event styling — for wavy, curly and coily hair.

The education half of that isn't an extra. What suits your pattern, how to section it, how to keep it on day three — that knowledge is what makes a cut last once you're home with it.
